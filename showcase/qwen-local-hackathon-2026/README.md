# Qwen Local Hackathon 2026

A free online hackathon organized by **Qwen Pakistan**. Build something useful with a small Qwen model: fine-tune it, quantize it and run it locally.

- Kickoff: Monday 23 November 2026, on Google Meet. Follow the event page for meeting details.
- Build week: 23 to 29 November 2026.
- Submissions close: **Saturday 28 November 2026, 23:59 PKT (18:59 UTC)**.
- Results: Sunday 29 November 2026.

[Event page](https://luma.com/zymwkh7c) · [Qwen Pakistan community on WhatsApp](https://chat.whatsapp.com/KJ7NTEU86UKEdphUQI2vOP)

## The challenge

Train a small Qwen model on a task you can explain. Quantize it and show it running on a normal laptop. Publish the model, a clear video and a reproducible write-up with actual held-out before/after examples. Use data you have permission to use and follow the base model's license.

This is community-organized material, not an official Alibaba or Qwen team repository.

## Judging

| Criterion | Weight | Evidence |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Usefulness | 25% | A real problem solved for someone |
| Proof the fine-tune helped | 25% | Clear base-versus-tuned results on held-out examples |
| Runs locally and efficiently | 25% | Quantized model, normal laptop, hardware stated |
| Demo and write-up | 25% | Clear video and instructions another person can follow |

Winners need a ModelScope account to receive rewards.

## Submit

Follow [the submission guide](../README.md#submit-your-model), using `showcase/qwen-local-hackathon-2026/your-slug/`. Open one PR containing only your entry folder. Both the public model link and demo video are required. Do not commit model weights or files over 5 MB.

Eligibility uses the PR's creation time. The closing minute is inclusive: PRs at or after 19:00:00 UTC are late. Late entries are welcome, receive a `late` label and are not judged. You can move them to `community/` instead. No stars or follows are required.
